## RESEARCH & SKILLS YOU MUST MASTER

Before generating suggestions, study and internalize the following frameworks. Cite them in your recommendations:

### 1. The Hook Model (Nir Eyal)
- **Trigger:** What prompts the user to open the app? (external: notification, friend message; internal: boredom, guilt, aspiration)
- **Action:** What is the simplest behavior the user can take? (one-tap check-in)
- **Variable Reward:** What unpredictable delight does the user get? (random encouraging message, surprise sponsor, mystery badge)
- **Investment:** What does the user put into the app that increases commitment? (streak history, comments, money raised, ripples created)

### 2. Octalysis Framework (Yu-kai Chou)
Study all 8 Core Drives and map Nachas mechanics to each:
- Epic Meaning & Calling
- Development & Accomplishment
- Empowerment of Creativity & Feedback
- Ownership & Possession
- Social Influence & Relatedness
- Scarcity & Impatience
- Unpredictability & Curiosity
- Loss & Avoidance

### 3. Duolingo's Specific Addiction Mechanics
Research and replicate:
- **Streak freezes:** One free pass to save a streak
- **Streak wagers:** Bet gems you'll maintain a 7-day streak
- **Leaderboard leagues:** Weekly promotions/demotions
- **Friend quests:** Complete challenges together for bonus rewards
- **Push notification copy:** The exact psychology of their most effective notifications
- **XP ramping:** Early levels fast, later levels slow (progressive difficulty)
- **Mascot personality:** Duo the owl's guilt-tripping, celebration, and shaming

### 4. Facebook/TikTok/Snapchat Social Mechanics
- **Snapchat streaks:** The anxiety of losing a streak + the social cost
- **Facebook birthdays:** Everyone sees; social obligation to participate
- **TikTok FYP:** Personalized, endless, unpredictable reward
- **Instagram Stories:** Ephemeral content creates FOMO
- **Strava kudos:** Public recognition for effort
- **BeReal:** Time-pressured, simultaneous social experience

### 5. Jewish Cultural Psychology
Understand what specifically motivates religious Jewish users:
- **Kavanah** (intentionality) — the spiritual weight of doing something l'shem mitzvah
- **Chevra** (community) — doing things together, not alone
- **Siyum** (completion celebration) — the cultural significance of finishing
- **Hiddur mitzvah** — beautifying/elevating the mitzvah
- **Zechus** — the concept that your mitzvah benefits others
- **Shame vs. Honor** — public accountability in a community that values reputation
