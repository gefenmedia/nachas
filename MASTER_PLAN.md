# NACHAS — MASTER PLAN & PROJECT DOCUMENTATION  *Last Updated: August 3, 2026*  ---  ## 1. WHAT IS NACHAS?  **Nachas** is a platform that fuses **personal spiritual commitments** with **charitable fundraising**.  Users take on spiritual challenges (e.g., tefillin daily for 90 days, Tehillim for 40 days, quitting smoking for 90 days, completing a masechta). Friends and family sponsor them **per day completed**, plus a **completion bonus** if they finish the full streak. The money goes to the user's chosen charity.  Every challenge creates a public **Nachas Page** where progress, streaks, sponsors, and encouragement are visible. When someone sees a friend's challenge, they can **take the same challenge themselves** — creating a viral **Ripple Effect**.  **Tagline:** *Turn Your Spiritual Growth Into Charity.*  ---  ## 2. THE CORE MECHANIC  ### The Per-Day + Bonus Model  This is the engine of the platform:  - A donor pledges **$X per day** the challenger completes. - The donor can also pledge a **bonus $Y** if the challenger finishes the full streak unbroken. - Example: $1/day for 90 days = $90. Plus a $10 completion bonus = **$100 total** if the full streak is hit. - If the challenger completes 85/90 days, the charity gets $85. The bonus is only unlocked on a perfect run.  **Why this works:** - **Not all-or-nothing.** The charity still gets something if the user stumbles. - **Motivates the challenger.** Donors want that bonus to hit, so they cheer the challenger on. - **Reduces anxiety.** A missed day resets the streak but doesn't zero out the fundraising.  ### Trust-Based Verification  For the demo and early phases, we operate on an **honor system**. The challenger self-reports daily completion via a simple check-in. Social accountability (public page, sponsor expectations, friend comments) is the enforcement mechanism. No photo verification, no GPS tracking, no fraud detection in Phase 1.  ---  ## 3. USER TYPES & FLOWS  ### Phase 1: Challenger-Only (Current Focus)  We are intentionally building **only the challenger flow first**. The donor/sponsor flow is lightweight and embedded within the challenger's Nachas Page.  | User Type | What They Do |  |-----------|-------------|  | **Challenger** | Creates profile → picks/creates challenge → picks charity → sets goal → shares Nachas Page → checks in daily → completes challenge |  | **Sponsor/Donor** | Visits a Nachas Page → pledges per-day or flat amount → no account required (name + email only) |  | **Charity** | Vetted organization that receives funds. For the demo, we manually curate 3-5 local charities we already know. |  ### Phase 2: Donor-Driven Campaigns (Future)  Sponsors will be able to initiate their own campaigns: pick a mitzvah, dedicate it (refuah, shidduch, l'ilui nishmas), pick a charity, and challenge their network to join.  ---  ## 4.
